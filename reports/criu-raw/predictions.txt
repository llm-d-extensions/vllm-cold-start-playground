TP=2: device footprint 146 GiB  ->  image ~165 GiB
  BUILD (off critical path) : cc checkpoint 60s + criu dump 273s = 333s
  RESTORE (critical path)   : criu read 46s + cc restore 64s = 109s
  vs cold boot              : 55s   ->  LOSS by 54s

Why this is the ceiling, not a tuning problem:
  A full-engine park replaces 'read ~62 GiB of safetensors, copy H2D' with
  'read ~83 GiB of criu image, copy H2D'. Both legs got bigger. The only
  lever that changes the shape is making the image SMALLER than the weights,
  which is what sleep level 2 does -- by discarding the weights.
